# Privacy Policy for Hunker Down Explorer

**Last Updated:** May 18, 2026

Hunker Down Explorer ("we," "us," or "our") is a fan-made, open-source project dedicated to cataloging  [Steve Wallis's](https://www.youtube.com/@campingwithsteve) camping locations. We respect your privacy and believe in keeping things simple. 

This Privacy Policy explains what data is (and isn't) collected when you visit our website.

### 1. Information We Do Not Collect
We do not collect, store, or process any personal data. 
* No user accounts or registrations are required.
* No first-party tracking cookies or analytics tools (like Google Analytics) are used to track your behavior on the site.
* The search, sort, and filter functions happen entirely locally in your browser. What you search for never leaves your device.

### 2. Local Storage
We use your browser's `localStorage` solely to remember your Light or Dark mode preference. This data is not transmitted to us or any third party; it stays entirely on your device.

### 3. Third-Party Services
While we don't collect your data, our site relies on a few third-party services to function and look good. When your browser requests resources from these services, they may receive your IP address and other standard browser information. 

These services include:
* **Google Maps:** We embed map iframes to show camping locations. Interacting with these maps is subject to Google's [Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
* **Google Fonts:** We use Google Fonts to style our text. 
* **Fav.farm:** We use Content Delivery Networks (CDNs) to load our site icon.

### 4. External Links
Our site contains links to external websites, specifically YouTube and GitHub. If you click on a link to a third-party site, you will be directed to that site. Note that these external sites are not operated by us, and we strongly advise you to review their specific Privacy Policies.

### 5. Changes to This Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time if the site's functionality changes. Any changes will be reflected on this page.

### 6. Contact Us
Because this is an open-source fan project, there is no corporate entity behind it. If you have any questions or concerns about this privacy policy, or if you'd like to contribute, please open an issue on the project's [GitHub Repository](https://github.com/moonbrooke/hunker-down-explorer/issues).
